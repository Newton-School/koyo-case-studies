## Overview

Urban Company is India's largest home services platform, founded in 2014 by Abhiraj Singh Bhal, Varun Khaitan and Raghav Chandra. It connects customers with trained professionals for cleaning, beauty, repairs and more, across India and international markets. Its Sales Consultants sell services face to face, so the role needs a strong pitch, customer handling and follow-through.

## The Challenge

- **Two Cities, Two Pipelines:** Bangalore and Hyderabad both hiring, campus and lateral
- **Panel Overload:** Every R1 sat on a panel calendar, qualified or not
- **Mixed Profiles:** Campus freshers and experienced sellers, one evaluation bar
- **Trust Gap:** Needed proof the AI's call matched the panel's before relying on it

## The Solution

**01 — Tanvi shared Urban Company's Sales Consultant R1 bar**
Sales pitch, customer handling and project management.

**02 — Koyo built a custom AI interview around that bar**
Same areas the panel checks, same expectations.

The panel met them too and logged both verdicts side by side.

## How It Works

**How did you set things up?**
Tanvi walked the Koyo team through what Urban Company looks for in a Sales Consultant R1: how candidates pitch, how they handle a customer, and how they manage their work. Koyo built the interview around that and sent invite links to campus and lateral candidates in Bangalore first.

**When did you realize this actually works?**
When the panel's comments started explaining the mismatches. In the first few interviews, Koyo tuned the evaluation and added maths as a skill. Agreement with the panel went from 64% to 80%.

**What surprised you?**
How much the panel's feedback improved the AI. Every panel decision went into a shared tracker next to the AI score, so the gaps were obvious and got fixed fast.

**Advice for someone evaluating an AI interviewer?**
Run it side by side with your panel before you trust it. Log both verdicts for every candidate. If the AI agrees with your best interviewers and you can see why it doesn't when it doesn't, you're ready to scale it.
