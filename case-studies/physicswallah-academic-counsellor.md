# How PhysicsWallah Screened 361 Campus Candidates and Closed 21 Hires — in 2 Days, Without Flying a Single Person Out

*PhysicsWallah used Koyo's AI Interviewer to run a full campus hiring drive at Chandigarh University for Academic Counsellor roles — no travel, no aptitude tests, no scheduling chaos.*

## Overview

PhysicsWallah (PW) is one of India's largest edtech platforms, founded in 2016 by Alakh Pandey. What started as a YouTube channel for physics has grown into a publicly listed company with 10M+ paid students, 80+ YouTube channels, and courses across JEE, NEET, and school-level exams. Their academic counseling team plays a key role in guiding students to the right programs — and hiring for it at scale is a constant need.

## The Challenge

- **Campus Screening at Scale:** 300–400 eligible students at Chandigarh University, no fast way to filter them
- **Bad Alternatives:** Aptitude tests can't evaluate communication or sales instinct — the things that matter for counseling roles
- **Travel Cost:** Flying a panel of interviewers to campus for 3–4 days, pulling them off active business
- **Scheduling Nightmare:** Coordinating 400 interview slots across a small panel — days of logistics before a single interview happens

*"Either we fly the team out for a week and lose them from business, or we run an aptitude test that tells us nothing about whether someone can actually talk to a student and close."*

— Shivam, Hiring Manager, PhysicsWallah

## The Solution

1. **Step 1:** Shivam tested the AI interview himself — impressed by follow-up questions, objection handling, and communication evaluation
2. **Step 2:** Shared his actual R1 questions with Koyo; custom interview built to match PW's counseling evaluation bar
3. **Step 3:** 361 students took the AI interview in a single day — on campus, on buses, whenever they were free
4. **Step 4:** Koyo delivered a ranked candidate list with detailed reports — communication scores, not just technical
5. **Step 5:** Panel of 4 interviewers conducted ~60 interviews the next day, working from the ranked reports
6. **Step 6:** 21 offers made. Two days. Zero travel.

## How It Works

**How did you set things up?**
Shivam shared the questions he typically asks in R1s for academic counselors — communication scenarios, objection handling, student engagement situations. Koyo built a custom AI interview around those. Invite links were sent to all eligible students at Chandigarh University.

**When did you realize this actually works?**
When Shivam took the interview himself. The AI asked follow-up questions he didn't expect, pushed back on vague answers, and the evaluation report scored communication quality alongside domain knowledge. That's when he decided to run the full campus drive with it.

**What surprised you?**
That 361 students completed the interview in a single day with zero coordination. No slots, no scheduling, no panel availability issues. Students who were traveling just took it from wherever they were.

**Advice for someone evaluating an AI interviewer?**
Try it yourself first. Take the interview. If the follow-ups feel real and the report tells you something you couldn't get from a resume, it works. That's what Shivam did, and that's what convinced the team.
