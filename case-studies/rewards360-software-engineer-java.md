CASE STUDY #2 — REWARDS360 × SOFTWARE ENGINEER (JAVA) HIRE

# How Rewards360 Cut 17-18 First-Round Interviews Per Batch with Koyo's AI Interviewer

*Rewards360 used Koyo's AI Interviewer to reduce manual Java screening rounds, save engineering bandwidth, and move faster on tech hiring.*

## Overview

Rewards360 is the team behind some of India's best-known bank rewards experiences, including HDFC SmartBuy, ICICI iShop, and Axis Edge. They power commerce, loyalty, and fintech infrastructure for large banks at scale. For a company hiring engineers into that setup, the first round had to be solid, but it was taking too much time from the people building the product.

## The Challenge

- **1-2 R1s a day** for Kalyan alone
- **60-90 minute** Java interviews
- Hiring managers losing focus time to weak candidates
- Team bandwidth going into repeat round-one screens

*"We knew exactly what the pain was. Good engineering time was getting spent on too many first-round interviews, even before we knew whether a candidate was worth moving forward."*

— **Anitha**, TA Lead

## The Solution

1. **Step 1:** Kalyan shared the exact Java round structure his team normally used: OOPs, Java theory, LLD, HLD, and DSA.
2. **Step 2:** Koyo built a custom AI interviewer around that format, tailored to how Rewards360 evaluates Software Engineer (Java) candidates.
3. **Step 3:** Candidates started taking the AI interview as their first round instead of going directly to hiring managers.
4. **Step 4:** Out of every **10-20** interviews, only **1-2** candidates now move forward for the next human round.
5. **Step 5:** Kalyan reviews the interview report, including the summary added after his feedback, before meeting a candidate.
6. **Step 6:** The report became part of the team's regular hiring workflow, so decisions happen with more context before the next round.
7. **Step 7:** As the results became visible, other hiring managers at Rewards360 started exploring the same setup for their own roles.

## TL;DR

**1-2 R1s a day. 60-90 mins each. 10-20 interviews in. 1-2 moved forward. 17-18 interviews skipped. ~10 final offers in 2-3 months.**

## How It Works

**How did you set things up?**
Kalyan shared the actual areas his team uses in a Java screen. Koyo turned that into an AI interview that matched their real first-round process instead of using a generic template.

**When did you realize this actually works?**
When the first few batches came in and only **1-2** out of **10-20** interviews needed a human round. That was the point where the saved bandwidth became obvious.

**What surprised you?**
The report quality. Kalyan could go into the next interview already knowing where the candidate was strong, where they were shaky, and what to probe further.

**What changed in the workflow?**
The AI interview report became a standard part of review before the next round. It is no longer just an experiment for one hiring manager; other teams have started adopting it too.
