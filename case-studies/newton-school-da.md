# How Newton School Hired a Data Analyst in 3 Days with Just 3 Hours of Manual Effort

Newton School used Koyo's AI Recruiter to go from 500 applicants to a signed offer in 3 days — for a role that usually takes a month to close.

## Overview

Newton School is an EdTech company founded in 2019 by Nishant Chandra (IIT Roorkee & IIM) and Siddharth Maheshwari (IIT Roorkee). They pioneered pay-after-placement programs in India and now run upskilling programs in data science, AI, and other in-demand domains. They also built Newton School of Technology — a new-generation tech institution that's been running for three years, with students already making waves in the industry.

## The Challenge

- Data Analyst roles historically took **25–30 days** to close
- TA team spending most of their time on resume screening, calling candidates, and scheduling — not evaluating talent
- Good candidates getting filtered out because their resumes didn't stand out on paper
- Hiring manager's time going into too many first-round interviews with unqualified candidates

*"We were screening hundreds of resumes manually, calling people, scheduling slots — and still ending up with candidates who weren't the right fit in the final round."*

— Pratiksha B., Talent Acquisition Specialist

## The Solution

1. **Step 1:** Job posted on LinkedIn. 500 applications received on Day 1.
2. **Step 2:** Koyo's AI screened all 500 resumes based on inputs from the hiring manager. 150 shortlisted. Zero manual effort.
3. **Step 3:** Interview links sent automatically to all 150 candidates. No calls, no scheduling.
4. **Step 4:** 67 candidates attempted the AI interview. 3 shortlisted based on performance.
5. **Step 5:** TA team reviewed AI-generated reports, picked top candidates for final round.
6. **Step 6:** Hiring manager interviewed 2 candidates. Loved one. Role closed.

## TL;DR

**500 applicants. 3 finalists. 1 hire. 3 days. ~3 hours of human effort.**

## How It Works

**How did you set things up?**
Devansh shared what mattered for the role — skills, experience, dealbreakers. Koyo used those inputs to customize resume screening and interview evaluation. Job posted on LinkedIn. Koyo handled the rest.

**When did you realize this actually works?**
For Devansh — when the final candidate walked in. Someone he would've rejected at the resume stage turned out to be the best fit. For Pratiksha — when 500 applications became 3 strong candidates overnight, without her opening a single resume.

**What surprised you?**
How little coordination was needed. 67 candidates interviewed themselves on their own time. No scheduling conflicts. No rescheduling. No no-shows.

**Advice for someone evaluating an AI interviewer?**
Let your recruiters focus on the decisions that matter. The screening, the scheduling, the back-and-forth — that's where the time goes. Free that up and your team moves at a completely different speed.
